A NOTE FROM THE REEF

How to adventure

Skille swims smoothly toward your mouse. On a touchscreen, drag your finger through the water. You can also use ↑ ↓ ← → or WASD.

  • Catch pink shrimp to grow your score.
  • Avoid bottles, cans, and plastic bags. A bump costs a heart. Skille briefly glows so you have time to swim away.
  • Keep exploring for ten minutes. Then a golden shrimp appears in the glowing arch. Catch it to win!

Pause with P or Esc. Your adventure also pauses when you leave this tab.
